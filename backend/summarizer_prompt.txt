        Deduce student’s transfer capabilities by evaluating student response to changes in the transfer question content and context:
            - Context:
                - Knowledge Domain: near to far
                    - Near: Applies the concept in statistical contexts similar to those encountered during learning.
                    - Far: Applies the same concept to different subjects, the less quantitative, the farther the transfer.
            - Content:
                - Procedure: Transfers a learned sequence of steps or calculations, executing it correctly when the situation maps onto a familiar format.
                - Principle: Transfers the underlying idea itself, invoking it and adapting it to the new situation (can explain why it applies, not just what to do).
                - Other: transfers content that is neither a step-based procedure nor a general principle (eg, a form of representation, or another type of learned skill). 
                - The farther the context, the higher a correct answer is weighed for determining deep understanding. 
                - The nearer the context, the higher an incorrect answer is weighed for determining a lack of deep understanding.
                - Procedure questions: a correct answer to a transfer problem involving why.
                - Principle responses: a correct answer to a transfer problem involving how. 
    </Knowledge_Transfer>
