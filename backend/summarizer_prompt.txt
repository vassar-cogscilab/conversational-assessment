<rubric>
    <Concept_Understanding>
        Aggregate the student’s response and evaluations for each concept to determine their conceptual understanding of each cluster.
    </Concept_Understanding>

    <Reasoning>
        Based on the overall conversation, how much did the student engage and thoughtfully explain their response to each question.
    </Reasoning>

    <Knowledge_Transfer> 
        Deduce student’s transfer capabilities by evaluating student response to changes in the transfer question content and context:
            - Context:
                - Knowledge Domain: near to far
                    - Near: Applies the concept in statistical contexts similar to those encountered during learning.
                    - Far: Applies the same concept to different subjects, the less quantitative, the farther the transfer.
            - Content:
                - Procedure: Transfers a learned sequence of steps or calculations, executing it correctly when the situation maps onto a familiar format.
                - Principle: Transfers the underlying idea itself, invoking it and adapting it to the new situation (can explain why it applies, not just what to do).
                - Other: transfers content that is neither a step-based procedure nor a general principle (eg, a form of representation, or another type of learned skill). 
                - The farther the context, the higher a correct answer is weighed for determining deep understanding. 
                - The nearer the context, the higher an incorrect answer is weighed for determining a lack of deep understanding.
                - Procedure questions: a correct answer to a transfer problem involving why.
                - Principle responses: a correct answer to a transfer problem involving how. 
    </Knowledge_Transfer>

    <Consistency>
        Judge whether the variation in the student's concept understanding ratings across turns is inconsistent, or can be explained by the difficulty of the questions.

        Stable — The pattern of understanding ratings is consistent under each cluster or can be explained by question difficulty/farness of transfer. This includes:
        Understanding rises across turns (eg, low → partial → correct), suggesting learning during the conversation.
        Understanding decreases as question difficulty increases or transfer becomes farther (eg: correct → partial → partial → low)
        Understanding stays consistently high, or consistently low, across responses.(eg: correct → partial → correct; low → low → partial → low)

        Unstable - The pattern of understanding ratings is inconsistent under one cluster and cannot be explained by question difficulty.
    </Consistency>
</rubric>
